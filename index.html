AI · Network Security · Trustworthy Learning

Machine learning for resilient networked systems.

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. Candidate at Fordham University. My research develops robust and efficient machine-learning methods for network defense, with connected work in trustworthy AI, computer vision, language, and multimodal intelligence.

I focus on systems that must operate under real constraints: noisy observations, changing attacks, scarce labels, privacy requirements, and limited compute. The goal is not only strong benchmark performance, but methods that remain useful when conditions become difficult.
