Discrete structures, taught for problem solving.

I taught CISC 1100, Structures of Computer Science, at Fordham in Fall 2025 and Spring 2026, helping students turn formal ideas into clear computational reasoning.

Teaching focus

Making formal ideas operational

Structures of Computer Science connects mathematical language to the habits students need for algorithms, software, data science, and security: defining terms precisely, reasoning from assumptions, testing counterexamples, and communicating a solution clearly.

01

Reason precisely

Move from definitions and assumptions to defensible conclusions.

02

Test ideas

Use examples, counterexamples, and small computational experiments.

03

Communicate clearly

Write solutions that make both method and reasoning visible.
